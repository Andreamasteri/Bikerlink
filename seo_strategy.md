# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public server-rendered marketing pages served by `server/site/routes.ts`
- Public informational pages: `/`, `/features`, `/sos`, `/motoclub`, `/community`, `/download`, `/about`, `/faq`, `/contact`
- Public matching explainer pages under `/matching/**`
- Public legal pages: `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/delete-account`

## Out of scope
- Authenticated Expo app routes under `app/**`
- Admin pages and APIs under `/admin` and `/api/admin/**`
- Web portal auth/account pages: `/registrati`, `/accedi`, `/area-utente`, `/media`
- Auth-gated trip planner `/pianifica`
- Mobile OTA/expo manifests and native app metadata

## Target audience
- Motorcyclists in Italy and Europe looking for riding partners, clubs, route tools, and rider safety features.
