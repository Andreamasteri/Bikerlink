How to Get Started
1
Open the app and tap "Accedi" (Login). Enter the email and password above.
2
Navigate to the Map tab (🗺️) to see nearby riders. The demo account is set in Florence, Italy.
3
Tap a rider on the map to view their profile and send a ride proposal (Proposta di giro).
4
Visit the Chat tab (💬) to see conversations. Matched riders can message each other.
5
Check Profile tab (👤) to edit profile, manage the garage, and view ride stats.
6
The Contest tab (📸) shows the weekly photo competition where riders vote for best shots.
